# Known limitations

- Thresholds are starting values and have not been clinically validated.
- Estimates are sensitive to lighting, camera position, skin tone, glasses, facial hair, and face-model behavior.
- There is no clinical validation or per-group bias evaluation yet.
- Blink and expressivity measures depend on what the bundled face model can track.
- The app is a monitoring and screening decision-support tool, not a medical device.
- Real-camera accuracy and mid-range Android performance need field validation.
